# Week 12: Final Presentations and Reflections  

## 1. Introduction  
This final session is dedicated to presenting the results of your final projects and reflecting on what we have learned during the blockchain study course.  
It is an opportunity to showcase your work, receive feedback, and share thoughts on future directions in the blockchain space.  

---

## 2. Final Project Presentations  

### Presentation Structure  
Each participant (or team) should cover the following in their presentation:  
- Project Title and Objective  
- Motivation and Background  
- Technologies Used (e.g., Solidity, Ethereum, DID standards)  
- Key Features and Implementation Details  
- Challenges Encountered and Solutions  
- Lessons Learned and Future Work  

### Presentation Guidelines  
- Time Limit: 5 to 10 minutes per presentation  
- Visuals: Use slides or demos to clearly illustrate your ideas  
- Focus: Highlight innovation, understanding of blockchain concepts, and practical applications  

---

## 3. Reflection and Discussion  

### Questions to Reflect On  
- What was the most interesting or surprising concept you learned during this course?  
- Which area of blockchain technology would you like to explore further?  
- What were the biggest challenges you faced when learning or building projects?  
- How do you envision blockchain impacting the industries you are interested in?  

### Group Discussion Topics  
- Emerging blockchain trends (e.g., DePIN, Modular Blockchains, zkEVM)  
- Ideas for continuing blockchain learning or project development after this course  
- Collaboration opportunities within the community
